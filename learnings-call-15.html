03

Test

New formats nobody else runs. Each one can run for a long time before the market copies it.

  • New humiliations. The airline makes her buy two seats, a skydiving weight limit, a bike taxi refuses her, she's mistaken for pregnant, a trainer, grandma can't keep up.
  • Song over a drama. Or a montage-style edit with music, like movie recaps. Reference: Les Misérables.
  • Same story, new skin. Retell a winning storyline with animals, babies or Lego.
  • Parody authorities. "Andrew Duberman", never a real name.
  • Solution-aware dramas. The pharmacy humiliation ad did about $1.5k in 17 hours. Try a fake vs real product drama too.
  • Blood sugar. A hospital patient drinks Ovia, the staff panic, and there's no spike.
  • #380 iteration. Make revenge the main payoff.
